About

Samy Naim.

Samy spent more than a decade working in the supply chains that move consumer goods from Asia to the US.

Bangkok. Hong Kong. Shanghai. Not on calls. In person, inside the factories.

01
What he kept seeing

American brands getting burned by sourcing that looked good on paper.

  • Agents who worked over email.
  • Factories picked by brokers who'd never set foot in them.
  • Quality checks by someone the buyer had never met.

The goods would land, and something would be off. Sometimes the kind of thing that costs a full PO.

02
So he built Namu

One non-negotiable: physical presence.

In every facility. At every stage of production.

03
How Samy works

Samy personally handles every client relationship. That's a feature, not a scaling limitation

— Namu's value is that you get a real person who knows your product, your tolerances, and your timelines. Not a rotating account manager.

One invoice. One point of contact. End to end.

  5. Final inspection

    Signed off before the shipment moves.
