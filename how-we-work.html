The process

Five stages. One point of contact.

  1. 01Discovery
  2. 02Brief
  3. 03Samples
  4. 04Production QA
  5. 05Final inspection
  1. 01

    Discovery call

    We learn what you're making, who it's for, your quantity, budget and deadline.

    You receiveAn honest answer on whether it makes sense, and how we'd do it.

  2. 02

    Sourcing brief

    We turn your file into a spec a factory can't misread: materials, Pantones, finishes, sizes and packing.

    You receiveA clear brief we agree on before anything is quoted.

  3. 03

    Suppliers & samples

    We find and vet factories in person, negotiate the price and produce physical samples.

    You receiveOne all-in delivered quote, and samples in your hands to approve.

  4. 04

    Production QA

    Pre-production review and inline checks on the floor while your order is being made.

    You receivePhotos and videos from the production line as it runs.

  5. 05

    Final inspection

    Finished goods are checked against your approved sample before the shipment moves.

    You receivePhotos, videos and a written report on request. Then delivery to your door, blind if you need it.

What you see from the floor
