FAQ

Good questions.

What do you source?

Branded and printed goods — apparel, promotional items, custom packaging, and branded hardware. Need something specific? Ask about a sourcing mission.

How do you handle quality?

By being physically present. We audit factories, approve samples, and inspect goods on the floor before they ship. One honest note: samples are often digitally printed, so color won't perfectly match production — that's why we run and approve test pieces with you before the full batch.

Do you work with small businesses?

Yes. Startup, boutique brand, or established company — we tailor the work to your scale.

Do you work with brand agencies?

Yes, it's a big part of what we do. We act as your production partner: you own the design and the client relationship, we make sure what you designed is exactly what arrives.
